I'm Buddy Johnson — second-generation baker, Navy veteran, IT guy, grandfather of nine. This is where the memoir gets written down, one dictated chapter at a time.

Who's Writing This

I've spent a working life going back and forth between two kitchens — one full of ovens and cottage-law paperwork, the other full of servers and support tickets. BITBShop is the name over both doors: baked goods on one side, IT consulting on the other. "From motherboards to pie crust, built from scratch" is the whole pitch.

Before any of that, there was the Navy, a bachelor's degree finished the hard way, four daughters raised, and a wife I lost. Somewhere in the middle of running a business I started writing it all down — not to publish a highlight reel, but to get it right while I still remember how it actually went.

The book is called A Life in Flour, Smoke, and Hard Time. This page is where pieces of it live while it gets finished.

The ferry is being drawn across the river by donkeys and mules, driven up Atchison's bluff street with a whip. Guess who was driving the livestock. The buildings in the background have long since washed away in a flood.
